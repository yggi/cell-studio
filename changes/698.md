### Added
- **A followed session's host is told each bar Cell studio didn't play** (#698): with a host of session version 2 (minor 2), the follower sends `lacked` for every bar it leaves silent or can't play (no score stated it, its digest isn't the one its score foresaw, a fragment it locks is missing, or it came to it too late), once per bar, at most 8 a second and no further back than 16 bars, so a Jambot soak can count its misses. A host of version 1 is sent nothing. Saved pieces are unchanged.
