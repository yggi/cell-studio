### Changed
- **Playing a long piece is lighter on the page.** The transport's timeline no longer restyles every one of its segments each time the playhead enters a new one (BWV 847's tempo map is about 1,100 of them, a new one several times a second); a wire, a wheel or a label is redrawn when a table it shows turns, not when any table in the piece does (each step of a selector redrew every one at once); the graph's cells and wires no longer stay layers of their own after they have faded in; the tower strip's ground (its rainbow, its scale and its names) is drawn once while the view stays, not every frame; and the wheels' slots, the edges' states and the views keyed by the turning tables are no longer re-checked every frame. Nothing played or drawn changes; saved `.cells` pieces are unchanged.
