### Changed
- **The library's wheel and style map are one canvas** (#695). Switching between Wheel and Style glides rather than cuts:
  - each piece both views show travels from its place on the wheel to its place on the map, and the zoom turns with it;
  - what one view alone has fades in or out, and the fragments fly out of the wheel's Fragments cell.
  Both views now zoom the same way: a pinch, ctrl + wheel, scrolling, and the − Fit + buttons. On the style map, a piece's title, or its wheel once grown, shows where it has room among the names, else it is a dot. The wheel also fits whole on a phone: its Fit now zooms out far enough to show all of it. Saved pieces are unchanged.
