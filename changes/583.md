### Fixed

- **A bar from a meter fragment numbers its beats again** (`&bar from meter_4_4 &tempo`, and any `meter(…)` bar since cells v0.18.0): its wheel shows 1 to 4 (6/8: 1 2, 7/8: 1 2 3) over its pulses, and its hub counts its beats, `4/bar` where it read `2/bar` (4/4, 5/4, 12/8). A `meter("2/4")` is drawn as its pulses, as every other meter is. Saved `.cells` pieces are not affected.
