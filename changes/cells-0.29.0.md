### Changed
- **The language moves to v0.29.0** ([its changelog](https://github.com/yggi/cells/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md)):
  - **Old spellings are errors now.** Before its 1.0.0 the language keeps no retired form: `trigger=`, `velocity=`, a card's `title=` and `tier=`, `over=key`, the bare swing and the others are errors that say how to write them now. Shorten no longer offers to move a card into the frontmatter.
  - **A style's axis is a sum of levels**, `&bite [&funk[bite] + &dsong + &dsect]`, drawn as wires into it.
  - **The funk, lo-fi hip hop and bossa nova drums take their levels from `metric_accent`**, a built-in fill: *Funk*'s rim strokes play at slightly different levels (88 of 577 events in 30 s, by at most .046).
  - **A written `seed=` is the seed alone**: the *chance* example's ghosts play 442 notes in 40 s, not 451.

  A saved piece that writes a retired form, or a `seed=` by hand, needs its new spelling or reads other dice.
