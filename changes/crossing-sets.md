### Added
- **A voice on several channels of one output, shown as one set** (webmidi v0.10): a piece whose MIDI cards put a voice on channels 1 and 3 of an output now shows it in the locked patch as one crossing, "1+3", where it showed only the first; the picker's *Several* chooses it by hand on any other piece, and a note is sent once on each channel, ascending. A piece with one channel a voice sends exactly what it did. Saved `.cells` pieces are unaffected, and the settings kept in this browser read as before: a set is written only where a piece or you ask for several channels.
