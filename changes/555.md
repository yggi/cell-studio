### Added
- **Patch cables dragged in the graph** (#555, decision 069 §4, yggi/cells decision 067 §4):
  - **Patch:** a tapped wheel shows its output on top. Drag from it onto a jack, and the cable is written into the text as cells' `patch` writes it: a factor (`lpf=70..4800 * &eg * &lfo`), at zero depth into a fixed parameter (`hpf=7500..7500 * &sweep`), a module's argument (`amp(level=&sweep)`) or a reference in a module's input (`[&saw, &sub * .5, &hat]`).
  - **While a cable is dragged,** every jack of the band it carries shows, lit, including the ones hidden at rest; the nearest within 22 px takes it, a finger's 44 px.
  - **Unpatch or move:** on the tapped wheel, a cable's end at its jack is grabbed (a dashed ring). Dropped on no jack, it is unpatched and the jack is back at its knob; dropped on another jack, the cable moves there.
  - **Refused drops:** a drop the text can't take writes nothing and says why where it was dropped: a jack that holds a cable, a value set on the line, the cell itself, a module's last input.
  - Each drag is one line rewritten, undone as any edit is. Saved pieces are unchanged.
- A cable into a module's inner product (`amp`'s `level`) is drawn into the jack it fills, which rung A left unmarked.
