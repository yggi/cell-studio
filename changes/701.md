### Changed
- **A meter's wheel shows its tempo, meter and swing** (#701): a bar written as levels (`from bar`, `meter("4/4")`) is drawn in the clock's white, not a hue a pitch or a voice would wear. Its pulses stand as tall as their level is strong (the bar's first reaches the rim), its beats are an arc round them, numbered, and a `swing=` is a ring inside: the swung level's pairs, a long arc then a short one at the share they play, a group of three kept even. While it plays the wheel stays and a hand goes round it, a turn a bar, the pulse and beat under it lit; the hub says the bar it is in (needs #710). Where the meter steps a selector that picks one section, the wheel takes the section's tint. A bar written as a scan keeps its old wheel, and the sheet's wheel is unchanged. Saved pieces are unchanged.
