### Changed
- **A filter is one biquad, as cells plays it** (#567, cells decision 074): a voice's or an instrument's low-pass (`lpf=`) is 12 dB an octave, where it was two biquads (24 dB) at its Q; `ftype=24db` plays two, as superdough does. A band-pass joins them (`bpf=`, `bpq=`, after the high-pass), and `ftype=ladder` plays its low-pass as two biquads, the ladder's slope but not its sound, until #589. **Saved pieces with `lpf=` sound different:** a resonant low-pass is softer and quieter, and 43 of the 83 examples move, most by under 1 dB of their average; Acid-Cell −4.8 dB, Moving −3.7 dB, the Prelude −3.1 dB, Patchbay −2.4 dB (its peak −11.5 dB, so it no longer clips: off the render check's known-loud list), Baustelle −1.6 dB, BWV 847 and Cellscape −1.1 dB (`tools/golden.mjs`, 12 s each; their cards are yggi/cells#553 and #523). The smoke test's resonant patch is within 0.2 dB of cells' render on its loudest block, where it was 7 dB louder.
