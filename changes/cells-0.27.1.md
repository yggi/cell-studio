### Changed
- **The language moves to v0.27.1** ([its changelog](https://github.com/yggi/cells/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md)):
  - **A meter's swing cell is drawn as a wire into its meter** (`swing=&swing`, as Jazz, Funk and every piece on the library's swung bar write it). It used to float unconnected, so 63 of the examples draw one more wire and lay out around it.
  - **Twelve examples that are one style's idiom say where they are found** on a new first line (`* from style=&funk`), so their lines count from 2.
  - **The chord reader names the minor seventh with a flat ninth and its half-diminished twin** (`m7b9`, `m7b5b9`).
  - **A form filled with a one-bar motif fits 4/4.**

  No saved piece plays differently.
