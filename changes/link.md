### Added
- **Measure link, in an output's details on the MIDI page** (webmidi v0.8.0): with a cable from an output to an input (or a device that echoes), press the button and 100 control changes (CC 119 on channel 16) go out and are timed back, giving the loop, the page's lag, jitter, loss and order, and a tier for scheduled and for played notes (the tiers are marked speculative until hardware numbers replace them). Nothing is sent until you press it; while it runs the probes heard back are dropped, so they are not forwarded, learned or played. The report is kept in memory only. Saved pieces are unchanged.
