### Changed
- **The built-in sound is a column of the MIDI page's patch, where *Built-in sound too* was a switch beside it** (webmidi v0.4.0, its decision 004): the first column, kept in the setup, so a setup can play MIDI only and another the built-in too; removed by its × or from its settings, and back with *+ Sound*. Its crossings show what the piece's own sound plays (a voice only on a MIDI card the piece marks silent isn't), and for now the setup hears it whole or not at all. The switch you had is moved into every setup once, so nothing you hear changes. With MIDI not connected and no built-in in the setup, the page says nothing is heard and offers it back. Saved `.cells` pieces are not affected.
