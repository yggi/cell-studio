### Fixed
- **The fragment browser** (#679). Saved pieces are unchanged.
  - **The map zooms itself.** A pinch, a wheel or a drag on the fragment map zooms and pans the map alone, and Fit shows all of it again. The page itself never zooms on a pinch any more, so no screen can be left zoomed with no way back on a phone.
  - **A card before opening.** A fragment tapped on the map opens its card first, as a piece does: one card for both. It shows a fragment's picture by its facet, its signature, its words, its facets, what it takes and which pieces and fragments take it (each a card of its own), and its text. From the card, Open or ▶ Play. A piece on the map opens its card too.
  - **The filter holds.** What the facet filter or the search leaves out is faint and can't be tapped.
  - **A search and names on the map.** The map searches fragments and pieces as the library does. Names show wherever they have room, more as you zoom in.
  - **Nothing overlaps an opened fragment.** Its bar and picture sit under the graph's buttons and left of the tower strip. The bar's ◂ goes back to the map it was opened from.
  - **A card on a phone sits over the tabs.** A piece's card no longer opens under the bottom tabs, which hid its Open button.
