### Added
- **Control panels on the MIDI page** (webmidi v0.5.0): panels of knobs, faders, an XY pad, buttons, switches, drum pads and keys, played, edited, made, deleted, opened and saved as `.midipanel.json` files from a *Panels* card under the patch. A setup names its active panels, each a row of the patch (routed to an output on a channel or as played) and a column (sent what an input or another panel sends); they are kept in this browser, and a first visit starts with two to try. A panel's row takes a **Plays** choice as an input's does, so a panel's keys and knobs play Cell studio's own voices (or the kit, or the armed cell) and MIDI learn takes them; a panel plays nothing there until it is chosen, and it is heard through the outputs it is routed to as well. Saved pieces are unchanged.
