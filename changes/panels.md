### Added
- **Control panels on the MIDI page** (webmidi v0.5.0): panels of knobs, faders, an XY pad, buttons, switches, drum pads and keys, played, edited, made, deleted, opened and saved as `.midipanel.json` files from a *Panels* card under the patch. A setup names its active panels, each a row of the patch (routed to an output on a channel or as played) and a column (sent what an input or another panel sends); they are kept in this browser, and a first visit starts with two to try. A panel's row takes a **Plays** choice as an input's does, so a panel's keys and knobs play Cell studio's own voices (or the kit, or the armed cell) and MIDI learn takes them; a panel of your own plays nothing there until it is chosen (the two seeded ones start on the default cell, so their keys sound at once), and it is heard through the outputs it is routed to as well. Saved pieces are unchanged.
