### Changed
- **The language is cells v0.18.0** (yggi/cells#520, [its changelog](https://github.com/yggi/cells/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md)). **A saved piece that draws may sound different, once:** chance (`x?`, `[a|b]`, `chance=`) is keyed on what its cell plays, not its name, so a renamed piece draws as it did but every piece draws anew at this version (yggi/cells#519). **A swing leans pairs only:** a group of three stays straight, so `swing=8n:…:pairs` says nothing more, and the meters' levels no longer show `pairs` (yggi/cells#498). A meter is two applications of `even`, and every meter name that read before keeps its grouping (yggi/cells#518). Written levels with a `-` gain a beat at it. New in the renderer cells keeps beside Cell studio: the echo, the reverb, grit, FM and an instrument's grit on its voices' sum, and filters as superdough's (`bpf=`, `ftype=`, the ladder). Patchbay is 1.5 dB quieter.
