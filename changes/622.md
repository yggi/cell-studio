### Changed
- **Editing a cell a `from` line gives makes it the piece's own** (#622, #610): turning or switching off a given cell writes its line under the `from` line, as before, and now also takes its name out of that line (cells' `takeIn`), so the line is plainly the piece's. Deleting that line drops the cell rather than bringing the fragment's back; write its name into the `from` line again to take it back. Saved pieces play as they did; a piece edited this way is written with the shorter `from` line.
