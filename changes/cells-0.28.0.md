### Changed
- **The language moves to v0.28.0** ([its changelog](https://github.com/yggi/cells/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md)):
  - **Four new tutorial pieces:** a band-pass, an advanced meter, Strudel's spelling and a table that comes round.
  - **More cells are drawn as wires:** a cell a mix reads by value (a level, or notes with their levels) is drawn as a wire into the mix. The fills of *daytime* and *5 to 12* are drawn too.
  - **Kinderlieder's parts** say where they are found on their own first line.

  No saved piece plays differently.
