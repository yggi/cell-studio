### Changed
- **An instrument's sends are a stage after its voices, as its filters are** (#566, cells decision 067): `room=`, `delay=` and an echo of its own (`delaytime=`, `delayfeedback=`) on an instrument (`&rev lpf=900 room=.3 [&saw, &sub]`) send what comes out of its filters, from each voice's way through it, and a moving one is moved by every note through it. A voice's own `room=` or `delay=` is its own send beside the instrument's, where the instrument's was lost; and a voice played both through the instrument and without it is sent through it, where it wasn't sent at all. A voice played only through an instrument whose sends it doesn't override sounds as before, but for a moving send, now moved by the other voices' notes through it too. No example's instrument carries a send at cells v0.16.0. Saved pieces may sound different where an instrument has `room=` or `delay=`.
