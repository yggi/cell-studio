# Changelog

What changed in Cell studio, newest first. The app is deployed from `main` on every push, so
this lists user-visible changes; each release (a version bump in `package.json` and a `vX.Y.Z`
tag) gathers the entries under *Unreleased* beneath its own heading. The version and commit of
the running build are shown in the piece picker's **Files** menu.

Format: [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/). Add a line to *Unreleased* in the PR
that makes the change. Also note changes to the language that could affect saved `.cells` pieces.

## Unreleased

### Added
- **Stats** (#390), a page of the sidebar: the open piece's numbers, live: cells by kind and in all, wires, nets, the braid steps each cell writes (min, median, mean, max), splices, characters and lines. A MIDI file's are those of the cells it plays through.
- **Strudel's names** (#337, decision 027): Strudel's and dough's other names for a parameter read as ours where they mean the same in the same units (`sound=` `cutoff=` `ctf=` `lp=` `resonance=` `hcutoff=` `hp=` `hresonance=` `verb=` `reverb=` `delayt=` `dt=` `delayfb=` `dfb=`; they were errors), the text keeping what is written, and Shorten offering ours where it is shorter; an alias beside ours is the parameter given twice. `scale("C:…")` reads every scale tonal knows, as Strudel does (`whole:tone`, `diminished`, `bebop:major`, `chromatic`, `in-sen` …). **`saw(n)`, `tri(n)`, `square(n)`** beside `sin(n)`: one cycle of a waveform in n steps, −1…1, as Strudel's bipolar signals. A *For Strudel users* section in the language overview. Additions only: saved `.cells` pieces read and sound as before.
- **Strudel's other step forms** (#337, decision 027: a shared spelling keeps Strudel's meaning), a lap of the cell its cycle: **`<0 2 4>`** one step a lap, in turn (nested, `<0 [2 4]>`, `[0 <2 4>]`, `<0 <2 4>>`; stacked with commas; `<0 2>*2`, `!2`, `@2` as in Strudel); **`x/n`** a step slowed over n laps (`[0 2]/2`: 0, then 2; a lone number bracketed, `[0]/2`, as `9/8` stays a fraction); **`x(k,n,r)`** a euclid inside a step (`&bd(3,8)`, `0(3,8,2)`, rotated as `euclid(k, n, r)`); **`[0 2|4]`** a choice of sequences (was an error). Each is one step to a pick, `shift=`, `base=` and `free(…)`. Additive: no example changes, saved `.cells` pieces play as before.
- **The tower layout's bottom row is in order**: the frame (`&key`, stepped by the bar at the sections' rate) left of its pill, then the bar, then the chooser (`&scenes`); a plain wire between two strata keeps at least 90 px of height, so the strata stand apart. Drawing only.
- **Four examples' sections are listed in the order the graph lays them out** (Hypercell, Pop, Zell, Brothers: a selector's taps, and the chooser's sections renumbered to match): every note is the same (checked over each whole piece). A saved copy of one of them keeps its own order and plays as before.
- **The tower layout's selector sits flat at its pill's bottom**: a hub, its arms to a fan of contacts (a tap each), two ports where its meter and its selection come in; the raw clock's bypass runs along the bottom. A table read only by cells over the selectors (a scale) is lifted to their stratum, and a bus's owner leads its net from the left, level with its readers; plain wires run as sigmoids. In both layouts a tag's wash takes the tint of the section whose tap steps its cells (a pastel shade of the rainbow). Drawing only.
- **Convert to cells writes a fugue in its voices** (#366): where a track holds several voices (a keyboard part's hands), its notes are given to voices and folded a line a voice where that is shorter, so a subject stays in one line whichever hand plays it. A voice that enters late in a long piece no longer writes a rest the parser refuses.
- The MIDI library plays its repeats (the Anna Magdalena pieces, Invention 6 and two WTC preludes were half their form: Mutopia's files play a repeat once; rebuilt from their LilyPond sources), and has a fourth collection, the Musical Offering's two ricercars. A bench for Convert to cells (`tools/disasm/`) measures it against what the typesetters wrote (#367).
- **A piece saved as MIDI opens with its own drums** (#334): a kit's track is named after its members and their pitches (`bd(A1) dundun(C2) shekere`), and Convert to cells reads such a track back as the piece's drums at their pitches, not General MIDI's stock ones. Eight more examples keep their notes through MIDI (jazz, five, korobeiniki, coltrane, bossa, bell, hypercell, the metronome). **A `glide=` run goes out legato** (MIDI out and a saved `.mid`): its notes, each held into the next by the slide, where it was one long note bent past the bend range; Convert to cells reads such a line back as a run on a gliding voice, so stereo keeps its notes too.
- **MIDI out sends a transposed kit member at its pitch** (#356, decision 029): a kit read beside a pitch term (`&theme * &orch`, a groove under a key) goes out as notes at their pitch, each member on its own channel (on a MIDI card, on its entry's channel), so an orchestration saved as MIDI carries the theme. Plain drums still go out as drum notes on the kit's channel. Saved `.cells` pieces sound the same.

- **A voice's own gain, `gain=`** (dough's): `&sd gain=.4 s=white …` plays the voice at that gain, times every gain it is played at (1 by default). It is how a kit's members keep their balance: `&kit &bd &sd` with `&bd gain=.8` and `&sd gain=.4` plays as one product a drum did. On the sound card `level=` stays the overall level in dB, on a gate how loud it plays. The old `gain=` (dough's, retired: a gate's level) now means this; on a gate written the old way (`trigger=`) it is still that gate's gain. Saved `.cells` pieces are not affected.

- **MIDI routing is a matrix, to any number of devices.** The MIDI page's list of voices and channels becomes a matrix: a row for each voice (and the kit), a column for each output, and at each crossing the channel the voice goes out on there, or *—*. A controller's drum pads play on every channel the kit goes out on, read back through that controller's own drum map. Bend range and MPE rotation are set per output, kept per device.

### Changed
- **The old selector of mixes is one clear error, and Shorten no longer rewrites it** (#261, [decision 027](docs/decisions/027-on-top-of-strudel.md)'s clean cut): `&song over=&parts` reading a table of mixes says what to write instead (taps of the bar), once per selector, without the "has no voice" or "a channel of the sound card is a mix" that followed it. A tap's line with steps or a table of its own (`&drumBar 0 1`) is one error, which lists what a tap's line may carry (off, label=, tags=, show=, size=, shift=, restart=), without "Unknown cell" for the other taps. A tap's sheet says what it is: "A tap of &bar on &parts: what it steps plays while &song has it on". **Saved pieces:** one still written with a selector of mixes (an error since decision 016) must be rewritten by hand; no example changes.
- **Nothing fails silently: more of what plays nothing, or is ignored, says so** (#195, #250). New warnings a saved piece may now show (none changes what it plays): `^n` or `^$n` where it moves nothing (`over=&k^1`, `step=&bar^1`, a line's name); `over=`, `step=` and the like on a gate, and a negative `level=` (or old `gain=`) on one; a table of taps no selector reads (`&parts step=&bar &verseBar …` alone: every part plays all the time); a melody of rests only (`&mel &e[0]` with `&e ~`); `shift=free(Eb, "…")`, a key by name given names of its own (ignored); `map=&card` or `map=&mix` (no numbers to map by); `x!n` on a card with one channel (`map=midi(3)`: each note sent n times); a plugin on a cell it doesn't fit (`show=sun` on a melody; from the layout, in the editor); a `loop=` or `step=` left `free(…)` on a clock nothing plays now has its slider capped, as a played one has (2,000 values a second: past it the planner failed). A `cycle=` over 50,000 is an error (it ran out of memory). Clearer errors: a card as another card's entry or mixed as a sound, an unknown cell in a product or a gate (was "has no voice"), a gate or a voice given to a gate's `level=` or `play=` (was "Two sounds can't multiply"), `&x^1^2`, `&x^1.5`, `free(0 2))`, `free(0 2)!3`, `rung=` (now "is tier="), a negative pick ("before the start"), the `shift=` warning at `shift=`, and no generated name for an unnamed cell. The editor colours `^$7` and a clock's `/2` as one modifier each, and offers `&x^1` and `&x^$7`; a tap's turn writes nothing; the time view names a level `&bar in 12` (was `&bar/12`, which read as a slower clock).
- **The drums' kit is wired simply**: each groove reads the kit by one wire, and the kit sends one sound wire into the mix, in place of a wire for each drum named and each groove's own into the mix. Drawing only. Over the library, the layout crosses less (drawn crossings 46.3 → 36.5).
- **A kit read only selects** ([decision 029](docs/decisions/029-kits-pass-pitch.md)): a pattern read through a kit (`over=&kit`, a groove of voices, a gate playing a kit) picks the member, and the rest of its product acts on it as on any voice. A pitch in the product transposes the member picked, a clock's `varispeed=` bends it (a kick on a tape machine dives with the tape), and a melody stepped finer than the kit read plays on the voice the read picks: `[&theme * &orch]` with `&orch over=&band step=&bar/4 0 1` and `&band &lead &organ` plays the theme a bar on each (it played the members at their own pitch, picked by the theme's numbers). Plain drums play as before; no example changes. **Saved pieces:** a kit read in a product with a pitch term, or on a clock with `varispeed=`, now sounds transposed or bent. MIDI out still sends a kit member as its drum note, untransposed.
- **Breaking: chance is keyed on the cell, the lap and the step, not the voice** ([decision 031](docs/decisions/031-chance-keyed-on-time.md)): a pattern's `?` and `[a|b]` draw the same on whatever voice plays it, renamed or moved onto a kit. Every piece with chance draws anew once; of the examples (their notes in the first 40 s), `baustelle` (1028 → 1051), `brothers` (284, other choices), `cellscape2` (80 → 87), `chance` (450 → 440), `focus` (79 → 78), `ghosts` (166 → 157), `onemore` (1281 → 1283), `resonanz` (145 → 142) and `testtones` (15 → 20). A saved piece with chance plays other draws.
- **Breaking: the sound card's level is `postgain=`, linear** (#150): Strudel's `postgain`, the card's master after the mix (1 by default, .5 about −6 dB), in place of the card's `level=` in dB. It can be a slider, `postgain=free(.8, 0..1)`; it takes no dB unit. `level=` now means only a gate's intensity: on a card it is a warning and does nothing. The built-in card's .8 is its postgain (its faders are at 1), so a piece with no card plays its notes at their own gains (MIDI out sends them a little louder) and sounds as before. A note's `about=` no longer keeps a sound off the built-in card. - **MIDI out** (#205): with an output chosen in *MIDI…*, every voice heard is sent to it too, timestamped for the moment the built-in sound is heard. Voices go on channels 1, 2, 3 … in the order the piece declares them (10 skipped; each can be changed, kept per piece in this browser), a kit's members on channel 10 under General MIDI notes picked by their names (`bd` 36, `sd` 38, `hh` 42 …), remapped per device (a *Novation Circuit* preset, or by hand). Saved `.cells` pieces are not affected.
- **MIDI in** (#207): a MIDI keyboard plays the piece's voices live, stopped or playing (a key is a hand, so a stopped piece is paused). Velocity gives the level, the sustain pedal holds; Pause leaves a key held sounding, Stop cuts it. *Thru* echoes the keys to the output on a channel; the computer keys (A to L, 1 to 7 a kit) play too when switched on (off by default; never while typing). Saved `.cells` pieces are not affected.
