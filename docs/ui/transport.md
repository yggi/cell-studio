# Transport

Play, pause, stop, seek and render: the controls in the header (`src/ui/Transport.svelte`).

- **Three states** (`src/audio/transport.ts`, a pure table with a test; `Player` carries them out): **stopped** (the initial state: time 0, no hand has touched anything, nothing recorded), **paused** (anything that is neither playing nor initial) and **playing**. A touch takes stopped to paused: a hand laid on a resonator (strike, damp, bow), a tape (scrub), a belt (grab) or a voice (a key pressed: [midi.md](midi.md#midi-in)) — all six go through `Player`, which calls `touch()` itself — a turned wheel, or the slider. Lifting a hand does not (it can be lifted after Stop); a turn moves where play starts. Stopped is kept, not derived from "time is 0": a touch at time 0 is paused. The pictures read two things: `heads` (where everything is at the transport's time, in every state: `docs/ui/graph.md`) and the state (stopped dims nothing). The design and what is still to come: [#201](https://github.com/yggi/cell-studio/issues/201).
- **The timeline by hand.** Not playing, turning a wheel that drives the piece (or the slider) moves where the piece is, forward or back, and everything follows: the playheads and tables (`heads`), every sound tape (a tape with a loop is frozen while held: nothing is written to it, and its lap goes round on its own, so turning it, by the timeline or by a hand on its reel, forward or back and round and round, plays that same lap over and over, as the rim of its reel shows it; a tape moved by hand keeps its displacement as the timeline moves), and a turn also sounds the notes it passes over, in either direction, as the hand reaches them — a tine rings as its pin goes by. The slider jumps: the tapes go there, no notes. At 0 the timeline stops, and turning on takes it forward again from there.
- **Play / pause.** Pausing keeps the place and lets go of everything in the air: a note that sounds drops its gate and goes into its own release (its bends and volume stream stand where they are; its FM modulators play on until the release has ended), a note scheduled but not yet started is never played, a filter held where it is. What rings after — releases, reverb and echo, a struck body — rings on (a held tape is silent unless a hand turns it), and a hand can still strike, damp, bow or scrub while paused. Opening a piece starts from rest: nothing sounds until Play.
- **Record** (●, shown for a piece with a braid left free, `free(0 2 4 ~)`) takes one pass of the armed cell from the keys and holds it in the braid's place until Save or Revert ([midi.md](midi.md#recording-a-braid-left-free)). It is a flag beside the three states (`recordTransition`: off, armed, taking), not a fourth: it starts play, and Pause or Stop calls a take off.
- **Stop** (the square) goes back to the initial state from anywhere: time 0, tapes wound off, hands' forces and turns gone, held `free()` values dropped (a take among them), every note cut, every body choked to rest and the output closed, so the effects' tails are not heard (a hand on the sound, or Play, opens it again). It is there when there is something to go back from. A stopped piece plays from the initial conditions.
- **A piece that ends** (`laps=`) pauses at its end, with what hands did kept; Play then goes on from the start. Stop, then Play, starts afresh.
- **The time slider** shows where the piece is and seeks: drag it to play from there. A piece that loops shows its cycle; one that ends (`laps=`) shows its length and the transport stops at the end ([../language/time.md](../language/time.md)).
- **Sections.** When a selector plays the piece, its sections are marked on the track and named by the selector's `label=`; each mark is a jump, and the current one is lit. On a phone the track sits below the title.
- **Tempo.** The tempo of the meter that drives the piece is shown, and is a slider when it is a `free()` value, as in the graph ([../language/free.md](../language/free.md)).
- **Render to WAV** renders the piece offline, faster than it plays, and reports its average and peak level in dB (`src/audio/render.ts`; the same `loudness` the console's `cellStudio.measure` uses, [../testing.md](../testing.md)).
- **The screen stays awake** while playing, where the browser has the Wake Lock API.
