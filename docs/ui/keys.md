# Keys and pads

An on-screen piano keyboard and drum pads, for playing live without a device (#255, part of the MIDI epic #203). They go through the same path as MIDI in ([midi.md](midi.md#midi-in)): `studio.keys.press(note, velocity, channel)` and `release(note, channel)`. Nothing new sounds and nothing is written into the code.

- **Where.** A dock along the bottom, under the graph and the code, so it never covers them. *Show the keys and pads* in the controls drawer (☰) opens it and closes the drawer; its × closes it. It is about a third of the height on a phone, and 170–250 px on a wide screen. It works stopped or playing; a key takes a stopped piece to paused, as every hand does. Whether it is open, its tab, the octave and the velocity setting are kept in this browser (`storageKey('cell-studio:dock')`). It is mounted only while open, so closed it costs nothing.
- **The armed cell.** Its name sits on the dock (`&tune`). Tapping it opens the same choice as *Play with* in the MIDI page (the same state, `keys.chosen`): a cell, or the open sheet's.
- **Keys.** Black and white keys from a C to the C that closes the last octave: one octave on a phone, up to four on a wide screen (`keyboardLayout`, `isBlackKey`, `keyRange`, `octavesFor` in `@yggi/webmidi/core`). − and + shift it by an octave, within MIDI's 0–127. A key plays the armed cell exactly as a MIDI key does: through its table to the nearest pitch, its tuning, A4, the sustain pedal and thru.
- **Lit.** A key lights in its pitch's colour (the palette, #34; C4 is octave 0, as in the piano roll) while it is held: here, by MIDI in or by the computer keys (`keys.held`).
- **Scale dots.** A dot in its pitch's colour marks each key the armed melody's scale can play: every pitch the melody can play, through its table and tuning, on the key nearest it (`engine/keys.ts` `scaleKeys`). Over 19-EDO a 7-step scale dots seven keys an octave. A kit, or a voice no melody plays, has no dots.
- **Velocity.** *Velocity* on: from where a key is struck, lower louder (32 at the top to 127 at the bottom, `velocityAt`). Off: every key at 100.
- **Sustain** is a toggle that acts like the pedal (`keys.sustain`). It also shows a MIDI pedal held down. Latched here, it comes up when the dock closes, so nothing rings on unseen.
- **Pads.** One pad per member of the kit that the kit channel (10 by default) plays (the armed kit, else the piece's first), named by its voice (`&kick`), in a grid. A pad sends its member's General MIDI note on the kit's channel (10, or the one the MIDI page's *Kit* row puts it on, on the first output) (`engine/keys.ts` `kitPads`, the notes `kitNotes` gives MIDI out). A pad lights while held from anywhere (`keys.lit`), and glows while the plan plays its member (the live state's `voices`, read only while playing and while the Pads tab shows). A piece with no kit shows no pads.
- **Fingers.** Each pointer holds the key it came down on until it lifts, is cancelled or loses its capture (`ui/fingers.ts`, Jambot's `Keyboard.svelte` pattern: capture in a try). So chords work on a phone, and no key sticks. Two fingers on one key strike it again; it lets go when the last lifts. When the window loses focus or the dock closes, every finger lets go.
- **Accessibility.** Keys and pads are buttons named by their note (*C4*, *F♯4*) or member (*&kick*), with `aria-pressed` while held. Enter or Space on a focused key holds it. The computer-key map from #207 (A to L, W E T Y U O, 1 to 7) is separate and keeps working beside it.

The code: `src/ui/KeysDock.svelte` (the dock, in `App.svelte`), `PianoKeys.svelte`, `DrumPads.svelte`, `fingers.ts`, `state/dock.svelte.ts` (the `Dock` in the `Studio`); the pure parts are the keyboard's layout (`@yggi/webmidi/core`) and `engine/keys.ts` (in `@yggi/cells`). The colours of the keys are `--key-white` and `--key-black` (`app.css`). The gallery's `keys` subject draws every state (`node tools/gallery.mjs keys`).
