# Architecture

What lives where in Cell studio, and how source text becomes sound. Language: [language/overview.md](https://github.com/yggi/cells/blob/main/docs/language/overview.md). Views: [ui/graph.md](ui/graph.md). Known gaps: [limits.md](limits.md). History and decisions: [MEMORY.md](MEMORY.md).

## Hard rules

- The code text is the single source of truth. Every graph edit is a rewrite of that cell's own line (`engine/edit.ts` in `@yggi/cells`: `setAttr`, `setFlag`, `setShift`, `setOff`, `setBraid`, `setTiming`; pure string to string, comments kept).
- `src/layout/` is pure TS: no DOM, no Tone. It runs in node under vitest. The language, `@yggi/cells`, is pure by its own gate, and imports nothing of the app (what is written is the language's, how it is drawn layout's); no module here imports itself round a cycle (`src/architecture.test.ts`).
- Theme values come from `src/app.css` and `src/ui/theme.ts` only (theme.ts mirrors app.css for colours computed in code; change both together).

## Data flow

```
code text (ui/state/piece.svelte.ts `Piece.code`)
  parse()              engine/parser/     -> ParseResult (AST types: ast.ts)
  compile()            engine/compiler/   -> CompileResult (cells, clocks, outputs, tapes, diagnostics)
  plan(result, shifts, muted)  engine/realize.ts -> Plan
  player.load(plan, source)    audio/player.ts
  every window: engine.window(from, 0.25)   audio/engine.ts -> engine/schedule.ts windowAt -> Window
               (plain data: Played[] — at, dur, voice, freq, bends, gains, levels, output — and moving tapes' speeds)
  retime(window, audioTime, secPerQuarter)  engine/schedule.ts: onto the audio clock
  voices.sync(result, shifts, muted)  audio/voices.ts: carries out wiring(result, muted) (audio/wiring.ts)
  voices.play(note)    audio/voices.ts (Tone.js; createVoices, parts in audio/voice/); the note names its output,
                       and opens the taps of the mixes it goes through (wiring's `taps`, audio/gate.ts)
  also: compile result -> writtenGraph (engine/graph.ts) -> buildGraph (layout/drawn.ts) -> layout/ -> ui/ drawing
        compile result -> flows (engine/flows.ts): where sound goes, read by routing and by wiring
        compile result -> routing (engine/routing.ts), liveAt (engine/live.ts) -> what the graph draws as sounding
```

The engine behind `engine.window` is local (in this thread, on the app's plan: the default) or a Web Worker (`?engine=worker` or `localStorage['cell-studio:engine'] = 'worker'`): `audio/engine.worker.ts` runs `engine/host.ts`, which compiles the same piece from its source, plans it, mirrors every hand (`grab`, `reset`) and answers windows by message; a window it doesn't answer within 40 ms is worked out locally. Measured, the worker doesn't lighten the main thread (scheduling is < 1 % of it; see [notes/15-engine-worker.md](notes/15-engine-worker.md)), so it stays opt-in; it's the message-shaped seam for moving the live state off the main thread later. Views read the sound through one `LiveSource` (`audio/live.ts`, implemented by `Player`): notes, spectra, waveforms, tapes, and hands (strike, damp, bow, scrub, grab).

`compileSource(src, env)` = parse + compile; the app compiles a piece through `src/compile.ts` (`compilePiece`), which hands cells the library its `use` lines read (the seed's, `resolveSeed`) and its file's lockfile; `withUsedLine` writes a used cell's line into the text, what an edit of it rewrites (#480). `codeShifts(result)` gives the shifts written in the code; live hand-turns replace them. `shiftsAt`, `clocking`, `timeMap` (clocking.ts) make every stepped cell's shift a function of time. `Player` (a 16th-note window scheduler on the Tone transport) swaps programs in whole at the next window. `renderOffline` (audio/render.ts) plays the same plan into a buffer.

## The language: @yggi/cells

Everything that was this repository's `src/engine/` and `src/theory/` is the package `@yggi/cells` ([yggi/cells](https://github.com/yggi/cells)), pinned in `package.json`: parse, compile, realize, schedule, the host, edit, Shorten, the folder, MIDI files, the time analysis; and with it the examples (`examples/*.md`, piece files: the title, tags and tier in the frontmatter, the piece in its cells blocks, its decisions 047 and 051) and the MIDI corpus (`midi/`). Its module-by-module map is [its architecture page](https://github.com/yggi/cells/blob/main/docs/architecture.md). The app imports a module by its path, `@yggi/cells/engine/<module>` (a folder's `…/index`), and reads the examples and the corpus from the package: Vite's globs over `node_modules/@yggi/cells/examples/` and `…/midi/` make each piece its own chunk, and the tests and tools read the same paths from disk.

What stays here of the language's tests is what reads the drawing: `src/layout/*.drawn.test.ts` (the cases of the engine's tests that build the graph as drawn) and `src/examplesIndex.slow.test.ts` (the library's index, its miniatures and each example's starts, chosen afresh; the build keeps the index by what it is made from, `tools/examples-index.ts`).

## src/session (a Jambot session's client side)

Cell studio follows a Jambot session as a client that listens (#478, rung 1), from the session contract (yggi/music-commons `contracts/session`, version 1, minor 3: protocol 16), not from Jambot's code. Pure TS, as `layout/` is (`src/architecture.test.ts`): `protocol.ts` (the messages, each host kind's fields checked: read, ignored or refused; what a lobby says; the version rule: any minor of version 1 joins), `clock.ts` (the host's clock from pings, Cristian's method: the shortest round trip of the latest 16 trusted, its half the bound; the host's heard moment is its page clock plus its output's offset, which each pong says), `follow.ts` (the bars' starts and the section texts the host sent: the bar heard at a moment on the host's clock, the text that states it, the told last of those that start at it or before, and where in the text's own time, bar for bar), `lobby.ts` (the link: `join` by the session's name, then `tell` and `said`, over a WebSocket; and a lobby watched, `watchLobby`: `list` every 2 s on a socket of its own that sends nothing else, nothing listed once it hasn't answered for 4 s; each session listed with whether its host is away, minor 3), `name.ts` (the name this browser joins by, “Cell studio” and a suffix kept in localStorage, and another offered when a host refuses one as taken), `rejoin.ts` (#482: when a dropped link tries again, after 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, then every 4 s, for 600 s, twice the contract's `resumeSeconds`; and the session taken for ended once the lobby has refused every try for 90 s), `feel.ts` (the performers' feel, from the feel contract, yggi/music-commons `contracts/feel` v1.1, #484, #492, and v1.2's reading, #511: each of cells' events' velocity and length as a host's performers play them, from the frame a host's `bar` carries, `frameOf`, and which part and drum role an event of a host's text is, `readerOf`: a pitched event's part the role of the performer whose gate plays it, cells' `performers`, else read off its output's id, `readingOf`; a drum hit's role its line's), `swing.ts` (the swing contract, `contracts/swing` v1: where a written step of a bar is played, and back), `perform.ts` (#492: one of cells' events read as Jambot's host reads it, its part and role, its note and velocity as MIDI out sends them on the host's channels, its step and length through the swing undone, and played with the feel: its level scaled to the feel's velocity, its end at its step and the feel's length, its onset never moved), `played.ts` (the log of what the feed played, as Jambot's `played()`: the latest 20,000). `vectors.json` is the contract's, copied with its `lifted` line; `protocol.test.ts` runs every vector of a client's two readers. `feel.vectors.json` is the feel contract's, copied the same way; `feel.test.ts` meets every vector, 7,140 events, none recorded, and the 17 outputs of its `reading`; `swing.vectors.json` likewise, `swing.test.ts` its 131. The state is `ui/state/session.svelte.ts` (`SessionClient`: join, pings, the welcome's version, what it follows, leave; a link that drops once seated, its socket closed or the host quiet for 3 s, is `rejoining`: it follows what it has and joins again with the welcome's `resume` token under its name, the same song's bars kept beneath the welcome's, and says so when the host gave another token, a new player's), and `Studio` follows it on the frame loop: the host's text is the piece (`Piece.follow`, read only: every edit refused), the cursor where the host's song is heard in it, so the graph's heads turn with the host's transport. The song is sounded (rung 3): `sound.ts` says what to play over a stretch of the host's clock, each bar from the text that states it, the text's own bar stretched onto the bar as the host lays it, where it has got to kept as a bar and the share of it played (so every onset is played once, none doubled or lost at a switch), a bar whose end isn't told yet (a host tells a bar's start a beat before it) laid as the bars before it went, a section's switch laid ahead of the work that holds the page, up to 2 s through the bars the host has told (`Player.layAhead`, #496, #513), and what it can't play said (`Lack`); `ui/state/sessionFeed.ts` (`SessionFeed`) compiles each text once and hands the player its windows, which `Player.feed` plays in place of the transport's run, each where the page's clock hears its time on the host's (the audio clock's pairing), on that text's own voices, each event as the performers play it (`perform.ts`, the feel of its bar's frame, which `follow.ts` keeps beside its start), and logged (`cellStudio.session.played`), MIDI out sending it too. A voice a section's text rebuilds or drops rings on until its notes have ended (`audio/voices.ts`, `retire`). Play silences and sounds it again; following starts it. The piece open when the host's text replaced it comes back when the session is left or ends. The library's lobby cell (rung 2, `ui/LobbyCell.svelte`: a disc beside the wheel, placed by `foldSides` in `layout/library.ts`, and a row above the cards) lists what `ui/state/lobbyWatch.svelte.ts` (`LobbyWatch`) hears while the library shows it (the picker says when: its tab, and the page not hidden), and joins a session chosen with `Studio.joinListed`, none whose host the lobby lists away (`LobbyWatch.joinable`).

## src/audio

`player.ts` (Player: transport, windows onto the audio clock, hands; implements `LiveSource`), `heldTapes.ts` (sound tapes while stopped: held where they stand, moved by hand), `device.ts` (the audio context: made as `tuning.ts` says, and what it reports to the performance overlay), `engine.ts` (`Engine`: `LocalEngine`, `WorkerEngine`, `chooseEngine`) and `engine.worker.ts`, `live.ts` (`LiveSource`, what views ask of the sound), `voices.ts` (createVoices, VoiceBank: builds the piece's voices, mixes, tapes and resonators and makes the connections `wiring.ts` lists), `wiring.ts` (pure: the connections `sync` makes, from `engine/flows.ts`, with `off` applied once: a connection needs its ends and every cell on its flows on; `wiring.test.ts` holds it equal to the flows for every example; and `taps`, which mixes' wheels each output's notes open: a mix's wheel hears what goes through it, #77), `gate.ts` (a mix tap that opens while the notes going through it sound), `voice/` (its parts: `voice.ts` a voice's chain and a note on it, `resonator.ts`, `tape.ts`, `noise.ts`, `params.ts` moving parameters, `nodes.ts` the typed bridge between Tone's nodes and native ones), `clock.svelte.ts` (reactive transport position), `render.ts` (offline render, WAV, `loudness`, `sampleHash`, `blockLevels`), `ticker.ts` (`onFrames`: the one frame loop for the transport clock and every live view, each at its own interval, eased under load; sound is unaffected).

Scheduling: Tone's context is made with `latencyHint: 'balanced'` (about 55 ms made-to-heard on the phone measured, `audio/tuning.ts`) and a 0.3 s look-ahead (0.12 s for pieces with hands, so a grabbed motor answers quickly); the sound itself runs on the audio thread, so a busy main thread delays scheduling (covered by the look-ahead), not the sound. The pictures are drawn from the position of what is *heard* (the audio clock held back by the output timestamp's lag, `audio/heard.ts`, read from the one estimate of the audio clock against the page's, webmidi's `clockOffset`, which MIDI out's timestamps use too), not of what is made. Live views refresh about 24 times a second (spectrum wheels 15, and only while on screen) from one shared frame loop (`audio/ticker.ts`) and stretch their interval when frames come late. Details and measurements: `tools/profile/README.md`.
